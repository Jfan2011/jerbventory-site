2. What Jerbventory is

Jerbventory is a local-first inventory and sales ledger for individuals who buy and sell collectible trading cards. It helps you record inventory, prepare for shows, capture sales, bundles, trades, purchases, fees, and expenses, reconcile what remains, and close out an event. With a Vendor subscription it can also list cards on eBay through your own eBay seller account and bring those eBay sales into your ledger (section 8).

8. eBay selling (optional)

What you authorize. When you connect an eBay seller account, you authorize us, through our eBay connector, to act on that account as follows. While eBay is connected, the connector reads the orders on that account automatically, including while the app is closed, so your sales reach your ledger. Each other action happens only when you ask for it in the app:

  • create, update, re-price, change the quantity of, and end listings;
  • mark orders shipped with the carrier and tracking number you enter;
  • cancel orders you choose to cancel;
  • turn on eBay business policies for that account, and create a ship-from location and standard shipping and return policies.

Never share your eBay password with anyone, including us; connecting always uses eBay's own sign-in page.

You can end this at any time with Disconnect eBay in the app, or by removing Jerbventory's access in your eBay account settings. Our Privacy Policy explains what the connector keeps and when it is deleted.

What stays your responsibility. You are the seller.

  • The accuracy of every listing is yours: title, description, condition, grading, photos and price.
  • So is complying with eBay's User Agreement and policies, and with the law.
  • So are your eBay fees, taxes, shipping, returns, refunds and dealings with buyers.
  • eBay may limit, suspend or close your eBay account under its own terms. We cannot change that and are not responsible for it.
  • Some actions have consequences on eBay. For example, cancelling an order as out of stock can count as a seller defect. The app warns you where it knows of one, but eBay's rules decide.

How syncing works, and its limits.

  • Sales on eBay reach your ledger after a short delay, not instantly.
  • While a lot is listed on eBay, the app holds its listed units so they cannot be sold again at a show or in the app until the listing's quantity is lowered or the listing ends.
  • eBay or our connector can be unavailable or delayed. When that happens you may need to manage a listing or order on eBay directly. Check eBay before relying on the app for a time-sensitive sale.

Vendor plan.

  • Connecting eBay and posting a new eBay listing need an active Vendor subscription.
  • If Vendor access ends, listings you already posted keep working in the app: their sales still sync into your ledger, and you can still ship, cancel, end and re-price them. Connecting eBay and posting new listings need Vendor again.
