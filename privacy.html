Jerbventory Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Applies to: the Jerbventory app for iPhone, iPad and Android, and the websites www.jerbventory.com and shows.jerbventory.com

The short version

  • Your ledger stays on your phone. Your inventory, sales, shows, photos and financial records stay on your device. They leave it only when you choose to do one of these:
    • export a backup;
    • post a listing to eBay from the app;
    • publish cards to a public show page (Community, below).
  • The app needs no account. Vendors who publish to show pages sign in with Apple or Google; collectors never sign in.
  • Prices. Card prices come through our own pricing service. It receives an anonymous device token and the catalog identifiers of cards you track or look at, never your records.
  • eBay. If you sell on eBay from the app, our eBay connector holds an encrypted authorization for your eBay seller account, the photos of listings you post, and the order details it reads from eBay. It never keeps buyer names, addresses or contact details.
  • Show pages. If you publish to a show page, our community service shows collectors the cards you chose, under the display name you chose. When collectors search, it uses their ZIP code or approximate location for that search only and does not keep it.
  • The website counts visits without cookies and keeps the email addresses people give us for a single launch message.
  • No selling or ads. We never sell personal information or use it for advertising. "Who receives data" lists every company that handles it.

What stays on your device

All of the following is stored only on your device:

  • Inventory and Collection records, including card details, quantities, condition and grading
  • Acquisition cost, asking price, market values and every calculated financial figure
  • Sales, bundles, trades, purchases, fees, expenses and show records
  • Transaction history, reversals and closeout results
  • Photos you attach to a Quick Capture, a card or a show
  • App preferences and settings

On Android this is a local database in the app's private storage. On iOS it is a local database in the app's container. Neither is sent to us. The exceptions are the ones you choose: eBay listings ("eBay selling") and show-page listings ("Community show pages").

Camera, photos and label scanning

Jerbventory uses the camera only for actions you choose:

  • attaching a photo to a transaction or card;
  • scanning a Jerbventory QR inventory label;
  • in versions that offer it, scanning cards into your inventory.

How photos are handled:

  • Photos attached to a Quick Capture, a card or a show stay on your device. They are uploaded only if you use them in an eBay listing or turn on Show my photos for a show-page listing. Both are described below.
  • Transaction and card photos are re-encoded before they are saved. That removes the image metadata, including any location the camera recorded.
  • Show photos are saved as a copy of the file you picked, with its metadata, including any location the camera recorded. They stay on your device and in your phone's own backups. If that matters to you, choose a photo without location data.
  • The QR scanner reads camera frames on the device only to decode Jerbventory's own labels. Frames are not saved, uploaded or used for card recognition.

You can revoke camera access in your device settings. Those camera actions stop working; the rest of the app keeps working.

Card scanning (test versions)

Test versions offer a Scan cards action for sports and trading cards. It works like this:

  • It saves each accepted photo and the text read from it in private draft storage on your device.
  • Drafts are excluded from backups and exports.
  • Photos are removed once their row is saved or discarded.
  • Nothing is uploaded for recognition. Text is read on the device: Apple Vision on iPhone and iPad, Google ML Kit on Android.
  • Google states that ML Kit does not send images or recognition results to Google. Its library does send API performance and usage metrics, and may contact Google for updates. See Google's ML Kit terms.

Our pricing service

Jerbventory refreshes card prices through a service we operate, hosted on Render. Store versions reach it at pricing.jerbventory.com; test versions use a separate test service run the same way. It exists so the pricing provider's credential never has to be inside the app, and so prices keep working offline at a show once fetched.

How access works. The app generates a random device token and registers it with our service. The token authorizes catalog and pricing requests and, on Android, Vendor billing checks. On iPhone and iPad it is kept in the system keychain. iOS can keep keychain items after an app is deleted, so the next installation deletes any token left behind before it contacts the service. On Android it is kept in the app's private storage, which uninstalling deletes. The token is not derived from your phone's hardware identifiers and is not linked to your name or email.

  • Automatic sign-up (store versions).
    • When. The app signs up the first time it needs prices: when you check for card catalog downloads, open a screen with catalog prices, or when the nightly refresh has prices to update.
    • Genuine-app check. Before registering the token, the app asks your phone's platform to confirm that the request comes from the genuine Jerbventory app on a genuine device: Apple App Attest on iPhone and iPad, Google Play Integrity on Android.
    • What happens to the confirmation. Apple or Google produces it under its own privacy policy. Our service checks it on our own server and then discards it; it is not stored or logged.
    • If the check fails. Automatic pricing stays off on that device, and you can still enter prices yourself. The app tries again later, at most four times in 24 hours.
    • Settings shows whether automatic pricing is on, and this device's pricing device identifier.
  • Activation code (testers). A one-time code we send you, which registers the token.

Limiting sign-up abuse.

  • Our service limits sign-up attempts from one network address per hour.
  • To do that, it turns your IP address into one-way codes, using a key that changes daily.
  • It keeps those codes only in working memory, never writes them to disk or a log, and erases them within 24 hours.
  • The IP address itself is not kept.

What is sent. When prices are refreshed (each night, when you ask, or when you open a catalog screen with prices), the app sends the catalog identifiers of:

  • the products you track;
  • on a catalog screen, the products shown there.

If the service cannot answer for every product you track, the refresh downloads the whole price guide instead. Those identifiers tell our service which products you follow or looked at. They do not include quantities, costs, asking prices, sales, show data, photos, notes, or anything that identifies you.

What we keep.

  • For each automatic sign-up:
    • a device identifier;
    • a hashed copy of the token;
    • the platform;
    • the date (not time) it signed up and was last used;
    • on iPhone and iPad, a hashed App Attest key identifier, its public key and a counter.

    These records are deleted automatically after 12 months without use.

  • For each activation code: its identifier, the device identifier, a hashed token, the dates it was issued, activated or revoked, and an optional device nickname we typed.
  • A sign-up in progress holds a random one-time challenge for at most five minutes.

Provider. Prices come from PriceCharting and, for sports cards, SportsCardsPro, under a written arrangement with PriceCharting.

  • Every price the app gets from them is labelled with its source and links to that product's page.
  • Prices you type or import yourself are never labelled as theirs.
  • Our service, not your phone, fetches prices from them, and they receive nothing about you from us.

Subscriptions and store services

The app offers Free access and optional monthly or annual Vendor subscriptions through Apple's App Store or Google Play. Your store handles checkout, payment, renewal and restoring purchases with your store account. We never receive your payment-card information. There is no Jerbventory account linking Apple and Google purchases.

On iPhone and iPad:

  • The app checks StoreKit transactions on the device and saves a compact entitlement summary in device-only keychain storage.
  • It does not send Apple transaction proof to our servers.

On Android:

  • The app sends your Google Play purchase token to our service, authenticated with the pricing device token.
  • Our service asks Google's Play Developer API to verify it, and acknowledges new purchases.
  • Purchase tokens and Google's responses are processed for that request only and are not stored or logged.

Google Play Billing includes Google's own diagnostics components, which may send diagnostic information to Google under Google's privacy policy.

Community show pages

Community lets collectors find Jerbventory vendors at card shows near them and search the cards those vendors brought, in the app and at shows.jerbventory.com. It runs on a third service we operate, the

community service, hosted on Render.

Collectors (no account)

Collectors never sign in. Finding vendors, opening show pages, searching and saving a wishlist work without an account, in the app and on the web. In the app you can also report a listing, block a vendor and mark a card "not here anymore". The web pages list our contact address for problems.

  • Your location. To find shows near you, you can enter a ZIP code or tap Use my location. Either way, it is sent with that one search and not kept.
    • "Use my location" shares your phone's approximate location, and only after you allow it in your device settings.
    • We never record where you are, and searches are not tied to a person.
    • The service keeps only per-show daily counts (such as how many searches a show had), with no device or person in them.
  • Your wishlist is kept only on your own phone, or in your own browser on the web. It is not sent to us.
  • A random device id. The app keeps a random installation id, not derived from your phone's hardware, and sends it with community requests.
    • On the web pages, the same kind of id is kept in a cookie named jvd. It is random, lasts one year, is used only by shows.jerbventory.com, and lets the service recognize the same browser for these purposes.
    • We store a hashed copy of the id only where it is needed:
      • to make "not here anymore" count once per device (erased within a day);
      • to keep the vendors you blocked hidden from you (until you unblock them, or the vendor deletes their account);
      • briefly with a report (below).
    • The service also counts requests per network address and device for at most an hour, using a key that changes daily, to stop abuse.
  • Reports.
    • What a report keeps: the reason you pick, any details you type (up to 500 characters) and what you reported.
    • For reviewing abuse: a hashed copy of your device id or network address is kept with the report and removed after 24 hours.
    • Deletion: once we have dealt with a report, it is deleted 12 months after it was made. Ask us and we will delete a report you made sooner.

Vendors (sign in to publish)

The ledger works without signing in. To publish your table and cards to a show page, you sign in with Apple or Google.

  • What we keep:
    • a one-way hash of the identifier Apple or Google gives us for you;
    • the public display name you choose;
    • your plan and free-trial-show status;
    • sign-in sessions, which expire after 90 days.
  • What we never receive or store: your Apple or Google password, your name or your email address. Google's sign-in on Android can include your email in what it sends; our service reads only the identifier and discards the rest.
  • A display name can identify you if you choose your real name. It can be changed once every 30 days.
  • On Android, Sign in with Apple runs in your browser and returns to the app through a verified link. The sign-in token travels in that link and is never stored or logged by our service.

What is public when you publish

Only what you choose to share:

  • your display name, specialties and "We're buying" lines, and your table number during show hours;
  • the show you are at, as described under "Shows" below;
  • for each card you publish: card name, set, number, variant, language, game, grading company and grade or condition, your asking price (unless you hide prices), how many you have or whether it is the last one, a spot hint and a back-stock flag;
  • a picture of the card from a public card catalog, if the catalog has one (see "The websites");
  • your own photos of your cards, only if you turn on "Show my photos" for that show.

Never published: cost, profit, market value, buyers, private notes, acquisition source, totals, Collection cards, scanner photos or unfinished drafts. A private location note reaches a public spot hint only if you copy it there yourself. Cards priced above your hide-above setting ($1,000 unless you change it) are not published.

Your photos. When "Show my photos" is on:

  • On your phone: the app shrinks each photo and re-encodes it, which removes location and camera details, then uploads it.
  • On our service: it removes any hidden metadata again, and shows the photo only while that card is listed and visible.
  • Deletion: a photo is deleted when any of these happens:
    • the card sells or you remove it;
    • you turn the switch off;
    • you leave the show;
    • the show ends;
    • you delete your account;
    • we hide it after a report.

When it is visible.

  • Cards and vendors appear from two days before the show.
  • Table numbers appear only during show hours.
  • Cards are hidden overnight, and everything leaves public view when the show ends.
  • Public card data is deleted 30 days after the show.

Pause, Remove me and Delete my account take effect when the request reaches the service. If your phone is offline, already-public cards stay up until it reconnects.

Shows

  • Who adds shows. A signed-in vendor adds the show they are attending: its name, venue, address, town, ZIP code, dates and hours, and optionally an entry fee, table count and link.
  • No review first. The show appears for collectors right away, without review. We can hide a show at any time.
  • After the show:
    • show details stay listed after it ends, so a repeating monthly show can carry on;
    • vendor tables and cards do not stay listed.
  • Organizer details. If you give us an organizer's contact details, we keep them private.
  • Organizer vendor lists. With an organizer's permission, we may list the vendors from their table list. A listed vendor can claim their row or ask us to remove it.

Deleting community data

  • Vendors: Delete my account in the app removes, right away:
    • your account;
    • your sessions;
    • your tables;
    • your public cards;
    • your photos;
    • your reports of a cancelled show;
    • any blocks collectors set on you.

    Shows you added stay listed for other vendors and collectors. Ask us if you want one removed.

  • Collectors can clear their wishlist in the app or browser, and clear the jvd cookie in their browser.

eBay selling (optional, Vendor plan)

Selling on eBay from the app uses a second service we operate, the eBay connector, hosted on Render and reached at marketplace.jerbventory.com. eBay's own credentials for Jerbventory live only on that service, never in the app.

Connecting. Connecting opens eBay's own sign-in page; Jerbventory never sees your eBay password. When the connector asks for it, the app first gets the same genuine-app confirmation described in "Our pricing service". The connector checks it, discards it, and keeps only which kind of check the device passed and when.

What the connector keeps.

  • Your eBay seller account: its eBay username, eBay's permanent user ID, and an authorization from eBay that lets the connector act on that account, encrypted with a key held only by the service.
  • Your phones: for each phone connected, a hashed copy of the app's random connector token, when it was first and last seen, and whether it passed the genuine-app check.
  • Listings you post from the app:
    • Kept: the identifiers that tie each listing to eBay, the random identifier of the Inventory lot, and its state and quantity.
    • Sent to eBay but not kept: the title, description, price and item details you write.
    • Also on request: if you ask, the connector turns on eBay business policies for your account, creates standard shipping and return policies, and creates your ship-from location from a ZIP code you enter. The ZIP code is passed to eBay and not kept.
  • Listing photos:
    • Where they go: the photos you choose for a listing are uploaded when you post it, and published at a web address eBay uses to show them.
    • Who can see them: anyone who has that address. eBay, browsers and other services may keep their own copies.
    • When they are deleted:
      • 30 days after the last listing using them ends;
      • 30 days after upload, if the listing is never posted;
      • all of them, when your eBay data is deleted.
  • Orders:
    • How often it reads them: regularly, including while the app is closed. That is about every 5 minutes while you have a live listing, and less often otherwise.
    • What it keeps, for each order line changed since you connected: the order, line and item numbers, quantity and sale amount, payment, shipping and cancellation status, and dates.
    • Never kept: buyer names, addresses, emails and phone numbers.
  • Actions you take: when you mark an order shipped or cancel it, the connector sends eBay the carrier and tracking number, or the cancellation reason.

The connector uses this only to publish and update your listings, ship or cancel orders when you ask, and bring your sales into your History. It is not sold, rented or used for advertising.

Disconnecting and deletion.

  • Disconnect eBay removes this phone's connection.
    • If no other phone is connected and you have no live listings, the connector also deletes the account's authorization, listing records, photos and order records.
    • If listings are still live, it keeps reading their sales until you end them and disconnect, or ask us to delete everything.
  • Delete my eBay data now deletes all of it immediately, even while listings are live. Sales of those listings then stop reaching the app.
  • Removing Jerbventory's access in your eBay settings stops the connector reading your account. It does not delete what the connector already holds.
  • Closing your eBay account: eBay notifies the connector, which deletes everything for that account.

The websites

www.jerbventory.com is hosted by Render, with Cloudflare providing network and security services.

  • It counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics. Cloudflare describes this as privacy-first, and it uses no cookies and stores nothing in your browser.
  • The Get notified form stores what you enter on our community service (Render). See "Launch notification list and notice at collection".

shows.jerbventory.com is our community service, hosted by Render.

  • It has no analytics.
  • It sets the jvd device cookie described in "Community show pages".
  • Card pictures: when a show page shows a card picture, your browser loads it directly from that card catalog's image server: Scryfall (cards.scryfall.io), the Pokémon TCG API (images.pokemontcg.io) or Scrydex (images.scrydex.com).
    • Like any web request, this shows your IP address to that server, which handles it under its own policy.
    • Our pages tell the browser not to send the address of the page you were on.
    • The Jerbventory app shows those same pictures only in versions that allow them.

Launch notification list and notice at collection

If you use the Get notified form, we collect the email address you enter, the phone platform you pick, and the date and time. It is used for one purpose: one email when Jerbventory is available to download.

  • It is not sold, not shared for advertising, and not connected to anything in the app.
  • That email will identify Jerbventory, include a valid postal address and a way to opt out, and opt-outs are honored within 10 business days.
  • The list will not become a newsletter without a new, clear choice from you.

Launch-list records are deleted within 60 days after the launch email is sent, or sooner if you ask.

Other network requests

  • Store versions use:
    • our pricing service;
    • our eBay connector, if you connect eBay;
    • our community service;
    • Apple's and Google's attestation, billing and sign-in services;
    • links you open yourself.

    They do not load card pictures from third-party image servers.

  • Some test versions (TestFlight and friends builds) also fetch card and set images and card data from third-party sources: TCGCSV and TCGplayer's image server, DigimonCard.io, the Pokémon TCG API, Scryfall and Scrydex. That lets testers see artwork.
    • Those requests contain card identifiers, not personal information. As with any internet request, the receiving server sees your IP address.
    • No version fetches prices from these sources; prices come only from our pricing service.
  • Traffic is sent over HTTPS. If your device is offline, the app keeps working and shows prices as unavailable rather than guessing.

Links that open outside the app

Jerbventory can open your browser for these:

  • at eBay or 130point, with a search you start;
  • at PriceCharting or SportsCardsPro, when you tap a price-source link.

These are ordinary links. Once your browser opens, that site's own privacy policy applies.

Who receives data

We share data only with:

  • Render (Render Services, Inc., United States). It hosts our pricing service, eBay connector and community service on servers in Oregon, and serves the website.
    • It processes data only to provide that hosting, under its terms and Data Processing Addendum.
    • It keeps ordinary connection records (IP address, time, page requested) for about 7 days.
    • It keeps daily backups of our services' storage for 7 days, so a deleted record can remain in a backup for up to 7 days.
  • Cloudflare, for the website's network, security and visit counting.
  • eBay, when you connect a seller account and list, ship or cancel through the app.
  • Apple and Google, for genuine-app checks, subscriptions and vendor sign-in, under their own privacy policies.
  • Card catalog image servers (Scryfall, the Pokémon TCG API, Scrydex). They receive only an ordinary web request from your browser when a show page shows a card picture.

Our own service logs record request methods, paths and outcome codes. They never record your IP address, tokens or sign-in confirmations. The price providers receive nothing about you. We never sell or rent personal information, and never share it for advertising.

How we protect data

  • All traffic between the app and our services uses HTTPS.
  • Device and session tokens are stored on our servers only as one-way hashes.
  • eBay authorizations are encrypted with a key held only by our eBay connector.
  • Vendor sign-in identifiers are stored only as one-way hashes.
  • Access to our services is limited to us and our hosting providers.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. If a security incident requires notice under applicable law, we will give it.

Tracking, Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

Jerbventory does not track you across apps or websites, and no other company collects information through Jerbventory about your activity over time and across other sites or apps. This covers Apple's and Google's own store and platform services described above, and the card-catalog image requests described in "The websites".

Because we do not do that tracking, we do not change anything in response to a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal. We never sell or share personal information for advertising.

Backups and exports

You can export a backup archive and choose where it goes, such as Files, iCloud Drive or Google Drive.

  • Our backups: the app's own backups contain your full app data, including financial records and the prices already recorded on your cards. They leave out the cached price guide and downloaded card catalogs.
  • Your phone's backups: your phone's own backups (iCloud or a computer on iPhone; Android backup and device transfer) copy the app's database as it is.
  • We never receive either. If you put a backup in a cloud service, that service's terms apply.

Treat a backup like a copy of your books, because that is what it is.

What we never do

  • Sell or rent your data
  • Show advertisements
  • Track you across apps or websites
  • Use your data to train anything
  • Require an account to use the app's core features

Children

Jerbventory is a business tool for adults who buy and sell collectible cards. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that a child under 13 gave us information, such as a launch-list email, we delete it. Email us if you believe that has happened.
